Chrome over a world

Glass composes with this because it is chrome, not ground. Bento, flat, skeuomorphism and hand-drawn all own their own ground — two grounds cannot coexist.

These frames are procedural, not photoreal. The shipping form of this technique is a chain of generated photoreal scenes: roughly N image plus 2N−1 video generations, mobile doubling the video count, about $27 for a six-scene 1080p chain. What is above demonstrates the mechanism and the layer relationship honestly at zero cash cost. ROUTER Gate 3.5 still requires confirming that budget before proposing the real thing.

No per-build generation cost at all; a WebGL bundle and a GPU budget instead. engine.md §6 applies: never mix GSAP or Three.js with Motion in one component tree, because they fight over the same frames.

The craft

It owns the ground layer. That is the real distinction from video scrub, and it is what decides pack compatibility: the frames above carry sky, horizon and light, so a pack that supplies its own ground has nowhere to stand. Chrome packs compose; ground packs are overwritten.

Same CSS mechanism as video scrub — stacked frames, one steps(1) slice each, no JavaScript. The cost difference is entirely in what the frames contain.

The cash cost is real and applies to the production form. Procedural frames like these cost nothing; a photoreal generated chain is ~$27 for six scenes, and ROUTER requires confirming that before the technique is proposed. It is the only item in the catalogue with that condition.

Implementation

/* identical mechanism to video-scrub — stacked frames, one slice each */
.worldwrap{ --n:40; --slice: calc(100% / var(--n));
  height:320vh; view-timeline-name:--world; view-timeline-axis:block }
.wf{
  position:absolute; inset:0; opacity:0;
  animation: wshow auto steps(1,end) both;
  animation-timeline: --world;
  animation-range: cover calc(var(--i) * var(--slice))
                   cover calc((var(--i) + 0.001) * var(--slice));
}

When not to use it

  • Without confirming the cash cost of the production form. ROUTER requires it, uniquely here.
  • In a pack that owns its own ground — skeuomorphism, neumorphism, flat, bento, hand-drawn, brutalism.
  • Mixed with Motion in one component tree, if using the 3D runtime form.
