01 — The rule

The backdrop is the style

Glass over a flat colour is a tinted rectangle. Everything above is looking through the same moving backdrop — that is the whole effect, and it is why parallax is this pack's core technique.

Why this panel is not glass

The budget is three surfaces per view and the nav, the hero and the pricing panel have taken them. A fourth would compound the blur and cost frames on every scroll tick.
