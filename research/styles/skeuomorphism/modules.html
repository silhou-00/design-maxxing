Skeuomorphism — Module Sheet

Token strip tokens.json

Don Norman calls this a perceived affordance — a visual detail telling you an action is possible. The modern register for it is hardware: a rotary knob affords grabbing in a way a leather texture never did. Two-tone is the norm — a light chassis carrying dark controls — with one saturated panel and a single light direction, top-left, across the whole device.

Rendered on purpose. The audit excludes .bad stages — a deliberate violation is a lesson, not a defect.

Banned the pack's hard filter

A pack's ## banned list outranks any vendor skill. These are mechanically checked where a browser can see them; the rest still need eyes.

  • more than one light direction on a screen — the single most common failure
  • flat solid background on an interactive control
  • pure #000 shadows at full opacity — always alpha, always soft
  • a raised look on something that is not clickable (false affordance)
  • screen-native accent colours like #0af or #f0f — they break the material illusion
  • icons drawn flat and then dropped onto a textured surface
