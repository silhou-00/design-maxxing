Modernism / Bauhaus — Module Sheet

Form follows function. Geometry, a strict module, and three primaries — nothing decorative has a role. Eight modules, five button states, dark mode and narrow width — every value read from tokens.json. This page is the proof; style.md and modules.md are the spec.

Token strip tokens.json

The module rule is the whole thing. Pick one unit — 64px here — and every shape, gap and column becomes that unit or an integer multiple of it. That is what separates Bauhaus from some shapes.

Rendered on purpose. The audit excludes .bad stages — a deliberate violation is a lesson, not a defect.

Banned the pack's hard filter

A pack's ## banned list outranks any vendor skill. These are mechanically checked where a browser can see them; the rest still need eyes.

  • gradients, shadows, texture, glow
  • more than the three primaries plus black and white
  • serif or humanist type
  • arbitrary angles — only 0°, 45°, 90°
  • decoration with no structural role
  • centred, symmetrical page layouts
