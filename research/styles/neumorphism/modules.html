Neumorphism — Module Sheet

One surface colour. Depth comes from a light shadow and a dark one, and nothing else. Eight modules, five button states, dark mode and narrow width — every value read from tokens.json. This page is the proof; style.md and modules.md are the spec.

Token strip tokens.json

Four numbers and one colour. Both shadows, always — one alone is a drop shadow, not an extrusion. And the 2026 correction: text darkened from the classic #4b5162 to #2f3542, because early neumorphism routinely failed WCAG and the sources say so directly.

Colour

--surface
#e4e7ee · ONE colour, mid-light. Figure and ground are identical - that is the whole illusion

--dark
#c3c7cd · surface x 0.87. The dark shadow

--light
#ffffff · surface lightened ~13%, clamped at white

--ink
#2f3542 · desaturated slate. Darkened from the classic #4b5162 to clear 4.5:1 - early neumorphism failed this routinely

--muted
#5a6274 · secondary text, still 4.5:1 on the surface

--accent
#3d4bd1 · colour lives in an ICON or in TEXT, never in the plate. Darkened from #4a5cf0, which is 4.16:1 on the surface and fails body text

Banned the pack's hard filter

A pack's ## banned list outranks any vendor skill. These are mechanically checked where a browser can see them; the rest still need eyes.

  • more than one surface colour - the illusion depends on figure and ground being identical
  • borders, outlines, dividers
  • any accent fill on the raised element itself - colour goes in an icon or in text, never in the plate
  • pure white or pure black surfaces - you lose one of the two shadows
  • dark neumorphism at low contrast - the light shadow has nowhere to go
  • text or icons inside a pressed well at body size
  • extruding every surface on the page - it destroys hierarchy
