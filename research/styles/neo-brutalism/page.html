01 — What we do

Four numbers, no exceptions

The difference between this and plain brutalism is the system. Every element obeys the same four values, which is what keeps the loudness legible.

2px

Border

Black or nothing. Grey is the low-contrast middle this pack exists to reject — and it is the single fastest way to make the whole thing look like a mistake.

4/4

Shadow

Zero blur. Zero spread.

5px

Radius

Small, not sharp.

×2

Hues, maximum

One accent carries the page. A second is permitted and spends the entire budget. There is no third — this section is already at the ceiling.

02 — The rule

The shadow is the affordance

Take it away and the thing is not pressable. That is why disabled controls here lose their shadow instead of fading — an opacity fade is banned, and a fade says "broken" where a flat surface says "not yet".

See all five states
03 — How it works

Stacking, hard-edged

The ● technique for this pack. A hard shadow makes the stack's depth explicit and literal in a way a soft shadow cannot. No-shrink variant — a scale() would round the corners optically and undo the style.

01

Each panel sticks at a hard offset

18px per panel, not a smooth ramp. The offsets are visible and countable, which is the point — the mechanism is not hidden.

02

The shadow does the depth

Because the shadow has no blur, a panel landing on the one beneath reads as two solid planes rather than a soft pile.

03

Nothing scales, nothing fades

Scale softens corners; opacity fades are banned outright. The panels arrive and stop.

04

Built from position, not JavaScript

This is position: sticky and nothing else. No scroll listener, no library, no measurement on every frame.
