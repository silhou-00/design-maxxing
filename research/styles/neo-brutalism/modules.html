1 · Nav / header 0 of 12 packs specified one

Full-width bar, no shadow at rest — a 4/4 shadow implies a floating card, and a bar spanning the viewport is not floating. It earns a shadow only once it sticks, and then only on y: a full-bleed bar has no right edge to cast from. That is the one sanctioned exception to global shadow direction.

2 · Hero where the copy budget bites

Engine §5 caps a headline at 8 words. This pack caps it at 6, because the display type is large enough that 8 wraps to four lines. No shadow on the hero container — shadows belong to things you can act on.

Design that refuses to whisper

Five words in the headline, twenty-two here. Both inside budget. The measure is capped at 56ch so the line never outruns the eye.

3 · Button — five states the module the set exists for

Focus is the state this pack nearly fails. The resting border is already black, so a black ring is a 0-pixel change and fails WCAG 2.4.13. An accent ring fails too, and not for the obvious reason: accent on white is 3.23:1 and passes, but the ring is drawn in pixels that were page tint, where it measures only 2.37:1. Hence a white gap then a black ring.

4 · Form field + error label, focus, and error without colour alone

Inputs carry no resting shadow — an input is a well, not a raised object. Only the submit button is raised. Without that distinction a form looks like a pile of identical bricks. The error border stays black: a red border alone is colour-only signalling (WCAG 1.4.1).

5 · Card the pack container, or its refusal of one

The pack's default object. Nested radius is calc(5px - 2px) = 3px. Cards in a grid need ≥8px gap — the 4px hover shift plus the 4px shadow needs that clearance or the hover reads as a collision.

6 · List / table density under the pack rules

Where this pack is strongest: hard rules are already its native vocabulary. Radius on the outer container only; cells are square, clipped by overflow:hidden. No zebra striping — the 2px rules already separate rows, and a tint would be a third value against the two-hue budget.

7 · Overlay / modal 1 of 12 packs specified one

Scrim is 80% black with no blur. Depth comes from the scrim, not from a bigger shadow — the temptation is to scale the shadow to signal elevation; resist it. The close button is 32×32 because icon-only controls need explicit sizing; padding alone will not clear WCAG 2.5.8.

8 · Empty / loading 0 of 12 packs specified one

The missing shadow is the message — there is no object here yet. Dashed is the one place the solid-border rule bends, and it bends deliberately: dashed reads as a slot. Loading uses flat blocks and no shimmer, because a shimmer is a gradient.

Dark mode 0 of 12 packs specified one

Not a background swap. A black border on a dark ground disappears — and in this pack the border is the entire affordance system. Border and shadow invert to white; the accent does not. And because the accent does not invert, text on the accent must not either — hard-code color:#000 or dark mode gives you white-on-blue at 3.23:1.

Banned the pack's hard filter

A pack's ## banned list outranks any vendor skill. These are mechanically checked where a browser can see them; the rest still need eyes.

  • blurred or spread shadows
  • gradients, glass, glow
  • grey borders
  • more than two accent hues per view
  • opacity-fade or colour-shift hover
  • border-radius above 8px
