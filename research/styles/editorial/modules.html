Token strip tokens.json

Display-to-body ratio is the whole style. Everything else follows from committing to at least 3:1 — a 46px display against 14px body. Match the sizes and you have a blog, not a spread.

5 · Card the pack container, or its refusal of one

There is no card, and that ban is load-bearing. The specimen shows the alternative: content separated by a column rule and space. A card grid is the exact opposite of an editorial spread.

Issue 04

The grid is an argument

A 42px display over a 16px body is 2.6:1, and the measure stops at sixty-six characters. Both inside this pack's unusually generous budget.

The grid is an argument

Display and body one step apart, which destroys the ≥3:1 ratio that defines this style, and an uncapped measure on top of it. The result is a blog post, not a spread — and no amount of warm paper or serif body will recover it.

Rendered on purpose. The audit excludes .bad stages — a deliberate violation is a lesson, not a defect.

Banned the pack's hard filter

A pack's ## banned list outranks any vendor skill. These are mechanically checked where a browser can see them; the rest still need eyes.

  • cards — editorial content sits on the page, not in containers
  • rounded corners and shadows on imagery
  • a single type size for headings and body — it kills the contrast that defines the style
  • sans-serif body text at long measure
  • centred body copy
  • stock photography
  • uniform, repeating section rhythm
